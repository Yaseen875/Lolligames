Wink Murder

Players take turns picking their role — everyone gets a lollipop if they're a victim, while one player gets a blinking eye (the secret murderer). The murderer winks people out one by one, eventually killing everyone, including faking their own death. The last person standing is the sole survivor and gets one chance to guess who had the blinking eye. If they guess correctly, they win; if wrong, the murderer wins.
